The rules.

  1. Five Cyprus half marathons

    Nicosia, Paphos, Limassol, Larnaka and Ayia Napa. Dates are in your passport.

  2. One international wildcard

    Any official half marathon outside Cyprus during the 2027 season.

  3. Submit your finish result

    Check in at the finish with the time from your watch, then add your official time and a link to the official results. A bib number helps us find you faster.

  4. Get verified. Collect the stamp.

    Every result is checked by hand, usually within a week.
