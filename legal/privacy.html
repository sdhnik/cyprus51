Cyprus 5+1 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 December 2026

1. Scope

This Policy explains how Cyprus 5+1 processes personal data when you visit the website, create a Digital Running Passport, upload evidence of a half-marathon result or contact us. It covers photographs, image metadata, GPS/GPX activity files and location data in particular. We act as an independent service, not as the organiser of the races featured in the challenge. Race organisers have their own privacy notices.

2. Personal data we collect

  • Account: name, email, account identifier, authentication and security records, account preferences.
  • Challenge and results: chosen race, event date, finish time, bib number where supplied, official results link, submission status, reviewer notes and earned stamps.
  • Evidence files: official results links and, where you choose to provide them, screenshots, documents or GPX files, with any associated metadata (which may include GPS coordinates, timestamps, device identifiers and camera information).
  • Optional finish photo: a personal photo you may add to your race page as a keepsake. It is not required to verify a result.
  • Community statistics: finish times, places and stamp counts of Cyprus 5+1 participants, used only in aggregated, anonymous form (see section 4a).
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device information, essential cookies, access and security logs.
  • Communications: messages and support requests you send to us.

Do not upload medical records, diagnoses or unrelated sensitive personal information. If such information is inadvertently supplied, we may remove or redact it where possible.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process account data and necessary result submissions to provide your requested passport and result-verification service (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract). We process reasonable security, anti-fraud and audit information to protect the Service and verify claims (Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interests, balanced against your rights). We process records where law requires it (Article 6(1)(c)). We use optional finish photos and personal GPX files to show them back to you on your own race page, as part of the Service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b)). Optional publication of your identifiable photo, detailed activity map or a promotional testimonial requires a distinct, freely given opt-in where consent is the appropriate basis (Article 6(1)(a)); declining such publication will not prevent you from earning stamps.

We do not use uploaded photos for facial recognition or biometric identification, and we do not track your live location through the Service. These are product commitments to implement before launch.

4. Photographs and screenshots — separate notice

This section covers images uploaded as proof of a result. Optional finish photos are covered in section 4a. A race photo or result screenshot can identify you through your face, bib number, name, background, metadata or a link to your account. We collect only what is necessary to review a result. Proof images are private by default: only authorised reviewers and necessary service processors may access them. We do not display proof images in public profiles, galleries or marketing material without a separate lawful basis and, where appropriate, explicit permission. Please crop out bystanders, other runners' personal details, home addresses and unrelated material before uploading. We aim to strip unnecessary camera EXIF/location metadata from display copies and restrict access to originals.

4a. Optional finish photo

You may add one finish photo per race to your passport. It is a private keepsake: it is shown only to you, on your own race page, and is not needed to verify your result. Reviewers do not view it as part of verification. Administrators have technical access to stored files only where necessary for support, security or a legal obligation. When you upload a photo, your device re-encodes and compresses it before upload, which removes camera EXIF data, including location. You can replace or remove the photo at any time on the race page.

4b. Anonymous community statistics

To show how you compare with other participants (for example, the share of Cyprus 5+1 runners who finished a race slower than you, or how many runners hold a given number of stamps), we calculate aggregated statistics from verified results of Cyprus 5+1 participants only. These statistics never show names, photos or individual results of other people. We do not import or analyse results of runners who are not users of the Service.

5. GPS, GPX and precise location data — separate notice

An individual GPX activity file may contain a chronological trail of latitude/longitude points, timestamps, altitude, speed and device information. When associated with your account, it is personal data and may reveal visits to your home, workplace or other sensitive locations, even if the route is labelled as a race. You are not required to provide a personal GPX track where a sufficient official results link or other less intrusive proof is available. If you choose to upload one (for example, for a race abroad), it is used as supplementary verification evidence, for security/anti-fraud review and to draw the course map shown only to you on your own race page. Raw activity GPX data and detailed timestamps are not made public by default, not used for advertising or sold, and not used for ongoing location tracking. You may remove irrelevant track segments and timestamps before upload when doing so does not prevent verification.

Public course maps: a generic GPX file describing an official race course is not automatically personal data merely because it contains coordinates. If it includes an individual's tracked movement or other identifying information, or is linked to an identifiable user, we treat it as personal data and apply these protections.

6. Review and third-party sources

To validate a claim, authorised reviewers may consult publicly available organiser or timing-provider results and compare identifying details (such as name, bib, event and time). We do not routinely send full private evidence files to an organiser. If exceptional third-party verification requires sharing personal data, we will limit the information shared and provide further notice or obtain permission where required.

7. Who receives data

Personal data may be processed by hosting, database, authentication, storage, email-delivery and security providers acting under appropriate agreements. At launch we expect these to include: GitHub Pages (website hosting; receives your IP address and technical request data), Supabase (accounts, database and file storage, hosted in an EU region) and Google (Gmail) (sending account and result emails from the project's own mailbox). Course maps load map images from CARTO (Positron basemap), which receives your IP address when a map is shown; map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO. Fonts are served from our own website, not from third-party font services. Data is also processed by authorised administrators who review race results, and by public authorities if legally required. We do not sell participant data. If these providers change, we will update this section. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we will identify and apply an appropriate GDPR Chapter V transfer mechanism and safeguards.

8. Retention and deletion

Proposed operational retention periods — confirm in the deployed system before publishing: account/profile, earned-stamp records and optional finish photos are retained while your account is active; pending proof files are retained until a decision is reached; accepted proof images are deleted no later than 12 months after the relevant season ends; personal GPX files used for your course map are kept while your account is active unless you remove them earlier (you can delete them at any time, which also removes the map) unless reasonably required for a documented appeal or legal obligation; rejected evidence is deleted within 90 days after the final decision; security and review audit logs are normally kept for 24 months; deleted-account data is purged from active systems within 30 days, while encrypted database backups are automatically deleted within 90 days, subject to legal retention requirements. We may retain anonymised, non-identifying aggregated statistics longer. Retention will be reviewed and shortened where feasible.

9. Protection of your data

We restrict evidence-file and photo access to authorised users, keep files in private storage, use encryption in transit, control access via role-based permissions, keep technical logs and review service providers. We do not knowingly place raw proof photos or personal GPX activity tracks in public storage buckets. No online service is risk-free; we will respond to security incidents in accordance with applicable law.

10. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies and similar mechanisms support sign-in, security and basic website operation. If we later introduce non-essential analytics or marketing trackers, we will explain them and request consent where legally required before enabling them. Any optional cookie categories and third parties will be listed in a separate cookie notice or consent interface. To work without signal (for example at a finish line), the app keeps a copy of the website files and your latest passport data in your browser's storage on your device, and a check-in made offline waits there until it can be sent. This copy stays on your device; your passport data is removed from it when you log out, and you can remove everything by clearing the site's data in your browser.

11. Your GDPR rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or objection to processing. Where we rely on consent you may withdraw it at any time without affecting processing already lawfully completed. You can delete your account yourself under Account → Delete account. Send other requests to the Administrator. We will respond within the periods required by law, usually one month, subject to permitted extensions and identity verification where necessary. You may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy) or another competent EEA supervisory authority.

12. Automated decisions and children

We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means. Stamp awards are subject to manual review in the initial Service. The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. You confirm your age when you create an account, and we do not knowingly invite children to create accounts. Contact us if you believe a child's information was submitted.

13. Changes

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the Service or law. We will communicate material changes through an appropriate notice.
